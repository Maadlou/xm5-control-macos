Design and build a world-class landing page for a macOS app called “XM5 Control”.

XM5 Control is an independent native macOS companion app for Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones.

The core idea is extremely simple:

“Sony made incredible headphones.
They just forgot the Mac app.”

XM5 Control fixes that.

The app gives WH-1000XM5 owners native control over their headphones directly from the macOS menu bar, without reaching for their phone.

IMPORTANT:
I am providing 3 screenshots of the real, working application.

USE THESE ACTUAL SCREENSHOTS AS THE PRIMARY PRODUCT VISUALS.

Do not redesign the application.
Do not invent fake UI.
Do not replace the screenshots with generic mockups.
Do not create fake Sony interfaces.

The landing page should frame, crop, layer, enlarge and present these screenshots beautifully.

────────────────────────────
DESIGN DIRECTION
────────────────────────────

I want this to feel like a genuinely great indie macOS product website.

Think:

Apple-level restraint
+
high-end indie Mac utility
+
beautiful audio hardware
+
a tiny bit of personality and rebellion.

It should NOT feel like:
- a SaaS startup landing page
- a generic AI-generated website
- a Sony corporate page
- a dashboard template
- a purple-gradient tech website
- a page full of cards
- a page full of meaningless feature icons
- a giant wall of marketing copy

Avoid the usual AI landing-page clichés:
- “Supercharge your workflow”
- gradient blobs
- excessive glassmorphism
- random glowing orbs
- dozens of floating cards
- giant rounded rectangles everywhere
- meaningless statistics
- fake testimonials
- pricing tables
- generic feature grids

The product itself should be the visual identity.

────────────────────────────
VISUAL LANGUAGE
────────────────────────────

Use a sophisticated dark interface inspired by the actual XM5 Control application.

Base palette:
- near-black / charcoal
- warm graphite
- subtle off-white typography
- muted Sony XM5 beige / champagne accent
- tiny amounts of green for connected states

The beige/champagne accent visible inside the app should become the signature color of the website.

Use it sparingly.

Typography should feel native, technical and premium.

Use a typography system close to:
SF Pro / Inter / Geist

Large headlines should be confident but not ridiculously oversized.

Use generous whitespace.

Use subtle borders rather than heavy containers.

Use very restrained shadows.

The page should feel especially beautiful on a MacBook Pro display.
